Artisan Craftsmanship

Our Handloom Manufacturing Process

A glimpse into how authentic hand-knotted wool and silk carpets are created on traditional looms in Varanasi.

Step 01

Handloom Knotting & Weaving

Traditional Loom Floor

Master artisans meticulously tie each individual knot by hand onto the vertical cotton warp foundation, building intricate patterns over months of dedicated craftsmanship.

Step 02

Artisan Yarn Preparation & Spinning

Natural Wool & Silk Yarns

Premium raw wool and hand-spun silk are expertly carded, dyed using rich natural color palettes, and prepared into strong yarn bundles for weaving.

Step 03

Carpet Washing & Finishing Stage

Final Luster & Softness

Once woven, carpets undergo traditional water washing, sun-drying, pile clipping, and final detailing to achieve a luxurious natural sheen and velvet-soft texture.
